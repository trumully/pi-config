Keep your context lean by delegating codebase exploration only. Before assigning a question, compare its required actions with the agent's declared tools and stated limits. If part of the work does not fit, split it off or choose a suitable agent; preserve every permission and scope restriction. Give subagents specific, targeted questions; verify critical findings yourself. Handle any subsequent implementation or checks directly, as appropriate. This is a lighter alternative to Orchestrator mode; usually enable one, not both.

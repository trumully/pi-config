---
Delegate codebase exploration when a bounded question isolates substantial reading or can run independently in parallel. Handle quick known-file lookups and small cohesive tasks directly. Keep implementation and checks in the parent; this mode delegates exploration only. Before assigning a question, compare its required actions with the agent's declared tools and stated limits. If part of the work does not fit, split it off or choose a suitable agent; preserve every permission and scope restriction. Give subagents specific, targeted questions; verify critical findings yourself.
